THE FAMILY CORNER

The yellow sign you look for on Court Street.

Martinez Grocery is a Carroll Gardens bodega that still runs like one: a counter, a beer case, flowers out front, and someone who will hold a plant until you can come back for it.

Come by for a sandwich. Call if you want it brought to you — free delivery is painted on the sign, not buried in an app.

FLOWERS & PLANTS

The sidewalk is part of the shop.

Cut flowers, houseplants, and the seasonal stand — pumpkins when the calendar says so.

  • Cut flowers

    Buckets by the door. Roses, mums, and mixed bunches that change with the week.

  • Houseplants

    Fiddle-leaf figs when they're in. If you can't carry it today, ask — they've held one before.

  • Hanging baskets

    The ones framing the green door, doing half the welcoming.

SHOP CAT

Mars keeps the sidewalk.

Neighbors stop for the cat as often as the flowers. Mars has been called a legend in print, which he has not disputed. He will show you a plant if he feels like it.
